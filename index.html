The system I model

Drag to rotate

A liposome with a charged lipid bilayer, a nanoparticle in its core, and an electrolyte on both sides. The code treats three regions (I: the aqueous core, II: the membrane, III: the outer solution) with different dielectric constants ε₁, ε₂ and ε₃, and solves for the ion density profiles and the electric potential.

From these I calculate the zeta, Donnan, diffuse and membrane potentials, the ion selectivity and the EDL charge.

Computational workflow

The self-consistent cDFT loop implemented in my code. It starts from the bulk densities and system parameters (ion and nanoparticle sizes, liposome radii and membrane thickness, electrolyte concentration, charges, temperature, and dielectric constants). The Euler–Lagrange equations are solved to obtain the output density profiles, which are then mixed with the input densities. This procedure is repeated until the convergence criterion |ρout − ρin| ≤ 0.01ρbulk is satisfied. Select the image to enlarge it.

Research experience

Sep 2023 to present

Research collaboration with Prof. Ezat Keshavarzi

Isfahan University of Technology

  • Wrote a classical DFT code in MATLAB (Solvent Primitive Model, MFMT hard-sphere term) for a charged liposome with an encapsulated nanoparticle. The Euler–Lagrange and Poisson equations, with three dielectric regions, are solved self-consistently until convergence. From the mean electric potential, I calculated the zeta, Donnan, diffuse, and membrane potentials; from the ion density profiles, I calculated ion selectivity and EDL charge under simulated gastrointestinal media.
  • Found that liposome charge and ionic strength strongly affect stability: anionic liposomes were more stable, particularly at lower ionic strength, and cationic ones more ion-selective. The size and charge of the encapsulated nanoparticle mattered mainly in neutral liposomes.
  • Compared cDFT predictions with published electrokinetic data to assess the consistency of the predicted charge-selectivity mechanisms and ion-distribution trends.
  • Developed a Kernel Ridge Regression (KRR) model trained on self-generated cDFT data to predict potentials.
